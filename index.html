Sunlong Social Inbox

三郎牧羊場（SunLong Goat Farm）的內部社群帳號管理工具。

功能與使用範圍

本工具僅透過官方 API 管理牧場自己的官方帳號，用於管理留言、私訊與排程貼文。功能以各平台支援範圍及實際授予的權限為限。

每則回覆都會先保留為草稿，等待牧場負責人核准後才送出。

Sunlong Social Inbox

An internal social-account management tool for 三郎牧羊場 (SunLong Goat Farm).

Functions and scope

The tool uses only official APIs to manage comments, direct messages and scheduled posts on the farm’s own official accounts. Functions are limited to platform support and permissions actually granted.

Every reply is kept as a draft and waits for the farm owner’s approval before it is sent.
