隱私政策

Sunlong Social Inbox · 生效日期：2026-11-07

1. 適用範圍

本政策說明 Sunlong Social Inbox（TikTok App ID：7705386213880367112）的資料處理。工具僅供三郎牧羊場（SunLong Goat Farm）使用，管理牧場自己的官方帳號。三郎牧羊場是臺灣彰化縣溪州鄉的家庭肉羊牧場。

2. 資料與用途

本工具透過 Meta Graph／Threads、YouTube Data API、TikTok Content Posting API 等官方 API，在平台支援與已授權範圍內，處理牧場官方帳號識別資訊、留言、私訊及相關識別資訊，以查看與管理社群互動。並非每個平台都提供留言或私訊讀取功能。

工具亦處理工作人員的回覆草稿、負責人核准資訊、貼文、影片及排程資料，以準備回覆及管理發布。回覆僅在負責人核准後送出。API 權杖與授權設定用於連接牧場已授權的官方帳號。資料僅用於上述牧場內部管理用途。

3. 儲存與分享

資料儲存在牧場自己的電腦，資料庫使用 SQLite。工具僅供牧場授權人員使用；權杖應保密，不應公開。資料不出售或分享給第三方。執行核准回覆與排程發布等功能時，必要內容會透過官方 API 傳送至對應平台。

各平台依其自身政策處理平台上的資料。請另參閱 TikTok 隱私政策、Google 隱私權政策及 Meta 隱私政策。

4. 撤銷存取權

帳號持有人可隨時透過 TikTok、Google 或 Meta 的帳號／應用程式設定撤銷本工具存取權或權杖。Google 授權也可於 Google 帳戶權限頁管理。撤銷將停止相關存取功能，但不會自動刪除先前儲存的本機資料。

5. 保存與刪除要求

本機資料用於牧場持續的內部社群管理。您可寄信至 goatfarmer524@gmail.com 要求刪除與您相關的本機資料，並提供相關平台、帳號或內容，以便識別資料；請勿寄送密碼或權杖。牧場將於收到要求後 30 日內處理。必要時可能需要確認請求者與資料的關係，以避免誤刪他人資料。

刪除本機資料不等於刪除 TikTok、Google／YouTube 或 Meta 上的原始內容；平台內容須透過該平台管理。

6. 更新與聯絡

如資料處理方式變更，將更新本頁及生效日期。隱私問題與資料刪除要求，請聯絡三郎牧羊場：goatfarmer524@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Sunlong Social Inbox · Effective date: 2026-11-07

1. Scope

This policy describes data handling by Sunlong Social Inbox (TikTok App ID: 7705386213880367112). The tool is used only by 三郎牧羊場 (SunLong Goat Farm) to manage its own official accounts. The farm is a family meat-goat farm in Xizhou Township, Changhua County, Taiwan.

2. Data and purposes

Through official APIs, including Meta Graph/Threads, YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API, the tool processes the farm’s official account identifiers, comments, direct messages and associated identifiers, within platform support and granted permissions, to view and manage social interactions. Not every platform supports reading comments or direct messages.

The tool also processes staff reply drafts, owner approval information, posts, videos and scheduling data to prepare replies and manage publishing. Replies are sent only after owner approval. API tokens and authorization settings connect the farm’s authorized official accounts. Data is used only for these internal farm management purposes.

3. Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally on the farm’s own computer, using SQLite for the database. Only authorized farm staff use the tool. Tokens should remain confidential and must not be made public. Data is not sold or shared with third parties. When performing functions such as approved replies and scheduled publishing, necessary content is transmitted to the relevant platform through its official API.

Platforms process data on their services under their own policies. See the TikTok Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Revoking access

Account holders can revoke the tool’s access or tokens at any time through TikTok, Google or Meta account/application settings. Google authorizations can also be managed on the Google Account permissions page. Revocation stops related access but does not automatically delete previously stored local data.

5. Retention and deletion requests

Local data supports the farm’s ongoing internal social management. To request deletion of local data relating to you, email goatfarmer524@gmail.com with the relevant platform, account or content so the data can be identified. Do not send passwords or tokens. The farm will handle requests within 30 days of receipt. Confirmation of the requester’s relationship to the data may be needed to avoid deleting another person’s data.

Deleting local data does not delete original content on TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta; platform content must be managed through that platform.

6. Updates and contact

If data handling changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact SunLong Goat Farm at goatfarmer524@gmail.com.
